# Before / After Example

A fully worked example calibrating the quality bar and the fabrication boundary described in `references/rewriting-guidelines.md` and `references/achievement-writing.md`. Use this to check tone and depth before finalizing real output — not as a template to copy structure from verbatim (see `assets/optimized-template.md` for that).

This example includes a case where clarification was asked and answered, and a case where a placeholder was the right call instead — both are realistic outcomes of the same workflow, not a "good path vs. bad path."

---

## Original Resume Excerpt

```
EXPERIENCE

Marketing Coordinator, Northbridge Retail
2021 - Present
- Responsible for managing social media accounts
- Helped with email campaigns
- Worked on improving customer engagement
- Participated in the launch of a new product line

Assistant, Campus Store (part-time)
2019-2021
- Handled register and customer questions
- Was in charge of restocking shelves
```

## Review Findings Used (from cv-reviewer, condensed)

- Impact: 32/100 — "Every bullet describes activity, none describe scale or result."
- Content Quality: 55/100 — "Weak, duty-focused phrasing throughout; 'Responsible for,' 'Helped with,' 'Worked on' appear repeatedly."
- Top improvement flagged: "Quantify social media and email campaign work if any metrics exist."

## Clarification Asked

> "Can you put a number on your social media work — follower growth, engagement rate, or post reach — and do you know the result of the email campaigns, like open rate or click-through?"

**User's answer:** "Instagram went from about 3,000 to 11,000 followers while I ran it. I don't actually track open rates for the emails, that was more the marketing manager's job — I just built them in the tool."

Note what this answer does and doesn't unlock: it gives a real, usable number for the social media bullet, and it *also* corrects scope for the email bullet — the candidate didn't own results there, only execution. The rewrite below reflects both.

## Optimized Resume Excerpt

```
EXPERIENCE

Marketing Coordinator, Northbridge Retail
2021 – Present
- Grew Instagram following from 3,000 to 11,000 through a redesigned content calendar and increased posting cadence
- Built and scheduled email marketing campaigns in [tool name, if worth specifying]
- Increased customer engagement through [Consider adding a measurable outcome here — e.g., response rate, repeat visits, survey score.]
- Contributed to the launch of a new product line, coordinating in-store promotional materials

Retail Assistant, Campus Store (Part-Time)
2019 – 2021
- Operated point-of-sale register and resolved customer questions during peak retail hours
- Managed shelf restocking and inventory organization for [Add scale if known — e.g., number of SKUs or store size.]
```

## Why Each Change Was Made

| Original | Rewritten | Rule Applied |
|---|---|---|
| "Responsible for managing social media accounts" | "Grew Instagram following from 3,000 to 11,000 through a redesigned content calendar and increased posting cadence" | Real number from clarification used exactly as given; weak "Responsible for" replaced with an outcome-led verb; method named because the candidate could describe it. |
| "Helped with email campaigns" | "Built and scheduled email marketing campaigns in [tool name, if worth specifying]" | Verb strengthened to match actual scope ("built and scheduled," not "led" or "ran") because the user clarified they didn't own the results — this is the "unsafe" pattern from references/rewriting-guidelines.md avoided: no invented ownership or metric. |
| "Worked on improving customer engagement" | "Increased customer engagement through [Consider adding a measurable outcome here...]" | No number was available and the clarification didn't cover this bullet — placeholder used rather than guessing, per the Placeholder Conventions in references/rewriting-guidelines.md. |
| "Participated in the launch of a new product line" | "Contributed to the launch of a new product line, coordinating in-store promotional materials" | "Participated in" is a flagged weak phrase; rewritten to name the specific, real contribution rather than escalating to a stronger-sounding but unconfirmed role like "Led." |
| "Handled register and customer questions" | "Operated point-of-sale register and resolved customer questions during peak retail hours" | Weak verb "Handled" replaced; "during peak retail hours" adds real context already implied by a retail role, not an invented metric. |
| "Was in charge of restocking shelves" | "Managed shelf restocking and inventory organization for [Add scale if known...]" | Weak phrase replaced with an accurate verb; scale placeholder added rather than invented, since no SKU count or store size was in the source or clarification. |

## What This Example Demonstrates

- A clarifying question that unlocked one strong rewrite (Instagram bullet) **and** corrected an assumption that would otherwise have overstated scope (email bullet) — clarification isn't just for adding numbers, it's also for keeping ownership claims honest.
- Two placeholders left in place where neither the source nor the clarification supplied enough to write a stronger line — resisting the temptation to invent "increased customer engagement by 20%" or a specific SKU count.
- Every verb change is traceable to a real change in what's known about the underlying work, not applied uniformly for "punchier" language.
