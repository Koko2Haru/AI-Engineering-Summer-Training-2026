# Intake Questions

Before writing feedback, gather context — a CV reviewed with no context is only half a review. Use these to fill in whatever the user hasn't already stated. Ask in batches grouped by theme below, not as a 20-item interrogation; skip anything already answered by what the user has shared.

**Always ask, by default.** Someone is always present in the conversation to answer — including a user answering on a fictional or test candidate's behalf. "There's no live candidate" or "this is just a test/fixture" is never a valid reason to skip straight to an assumption; if that reasoning shows up, it's a bug, not a legitimate shortcut. The only legitimate skip is the user *declining* to answer after being asked — saying "just review it," "skip the questions," or not responding — at which point proceed on a stated assumption and note which questions are still open (see `resume-writing-guide.md` / the skill's Input Expectations for how to phrase an assumption).

## About the person

1. Degree, university, graduation year?
2. GPA — and are they comfortable showing it?
3. Current year of study (if a student)?
4. Nationality and target job market (Saudi Arabia, US, UK, global)?
5. Languages spoken, and at what level?

## About the target

6. What specific role or role type is this targeting (AI engineer, consultant, PM, data scientist, SWE, research intern — be specific, not "tech")?
7. Is this for one specific application, or a general CV sent to many places?
8. What companies or types of companies are being targeted?
9. Going through an ATS, or mostly direct/cold outreach?
10. Target seniority (intern, junior, mid, senior, leadership program)?

## About the content

11. For each experience — what did the candidate actually do day to day? Not the title; what was built, fixed, designed, led, or delivered?
12. For every number/metric already on the CV — how confident is the candidate in it? Measured, estimated, or guessed?
13. Is there anything on the CV the candidate couldn't defend if asked about it directly in an interview?
14. What's missing that the candidate has actually done but left out — and why was it left out?
15. Is there anything inflated or made to sound better than it was?

## About the CV itself

16. What format is this in — self-designed or a template?
17. Has anyone reviewed this before? What feedback came back?
18. How long has this version been in circulation? Any interviews from it?
19. Target length — one page, two pages, no preference?
20. Any section the candidate already feels unsure about or wants focused on specifically?

## How to use these

- Ask all at once or in themed batches — whichever fits the conversation. Don't drip-feed one question at a time when several are needed.
- Prioritize by what would most change the review: target role (Q6) and content confidence (Q12-13) usually matter more than format history (Q16-18).
- If answers reveal a claim the candidate can't defend (Q13, Q15), that's a Credibility Audit finding — see `bullet-audits.md`.
- Missing answers are not a blocker. Proceed with a stated assumption and flag which questions are still open so the user can fill them in later.
